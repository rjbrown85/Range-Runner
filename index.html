RangeRunner

Pick a scale or one of your licks, set your lowest and highest note, and press play. A one-bar count-in starts the click, and the click never stops. Each round lands the chord on a downbeat, you sing the pattern, and the click plays out the rest of the bar before the next key comes in. The run climbs to the top of your range and walks back down.

Edit

Keyboard: click the tab first. Numbers type the fret (two quick digits make 10 to 24), ← → move, ↑ ↓ change string, + − change length, . dotted, 3 with Shift for triplet, L tie, R rest, Insert or I new beat, Delete removes the beat, Backspace clears the note, Ctrl+Z undo.

String + fret
E A D G B e, low to high. Lowercase e is the high string: G7 = G string, 7th fret.
Length after a colon
w whole, h half, q quarter, e eighth, s sixteenth, x 32nd. Add . for dotted, 3 for triplet: e3.
Ties
B2:e+q holds one note for an eighth plus a quarter. ~:q holds the note before it, like (2) in tab.
Rests and bars
r:e is an eighth rest. | bar lines are optional. Leave off the length to repeat the last one.
